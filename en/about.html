03 — Our story and vision

About HK Studio

HK Studio is a digital-innovation startup studio based in Bingerville. We design, launch and scale artificial intelligence, data and economic-intelligence solutions for African enterprises.

Who we are

A digital-innovation studio, at the heart of Bingerville.

HK Studio supports West-African companies and founders through their digital transformation. We combine artificial intelligence, data engineering and economic intelligence to turn ideas into concrete, profitable products.

Our belief is simple: Africa should not merely consume the technologies transforming the world — it should build them. That is why we operate as a studio: we commit for the long run, from the first idea to market launch.

From strategy to web development, from process automation to AI-model deployment, we put technology to work toward a single goal: measurable results.

— Konan Hervé Serge · Founder & CEO

Our guiding principles

03
01

ROI Obsession

Every project must create measurable value: time saved, revenue up, or a costly error removed.

In practice

Before any quote, we quantify the expected impact together and set a clear target. If the project won't pay for itself quickly, we tell you — in writing, in our proposal.

02

Zero Lock-In

What we build belongs to you. We train your teams so you stay in control of your tools and your data.

In practice

At handover, we give you full access, clear documentation and onboarding. Everything runs on YOUR accounts, never ours — you stay free.

03

Local Roots

Based in Bingerville, we know your markets, languages and realities. We build for the African context.

In practice

We come to your offices for audits and training. A dedicated channel connects you to your lead, with a first reply within 2 business hours.

04

Demanding Simplicity

We favour direct, robust solutions without over-engineering — fewer failures, controlled costs, a solution your team understands.

In practice

We prefer a clean architecture over a fragile machine. Fewer moving parts means more reliability and calmer maintenance over time.

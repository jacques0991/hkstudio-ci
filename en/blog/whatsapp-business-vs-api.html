WhatsApp Business or WhatsApp API: which one should your SME choose?

It is the question every business owner asks when automating their main sales channel: stick with the free WhatsApp Business app, or move to the WhatsApp Business API? The answer depends on your conversation volume and what you want to automate. Here is how to decide in five minutes.

WhatsApp Business: free, simple, but limited

The WhatsApp Business app (the one from the Play Store) already offers real professional tools: business profile, product catalog, automatic greeting, away message, quick replies and labels to sort conversations.

Its structural limits: one main user (multi-device remains limited), no real connection to a CRM or external software, no large-scale follow-up campaigns, and static auto-replies — the greeting message does not understand the customer's question, it merely receives it.

The WhatsApp Business API: the engine of serious systems

The API is not an app: it is a technical gateway to the WhatsApp network, onto which systems are plugged. Concretely, it enables:

  • an AI agent that replies in natural language, 24/7, based on your real products and prices;
  • several operators on the same number, with conversation routing;
  • CRM connection: every conversation automatically feeds the customer record;
  • automatic follow-ups and outbound campaigns (message templates approved by Meta);
  • real statistics: volume, response time, conversion rate.

In return: it is paid (Meta bills per conversation), and it is set up with a specialist provider or platform.

The decision criterion in one sentence

Fewer than 20–30 conversations a day and a single salesperson: the free app, well configured, is enough. Beyond that, or as soon as you want CRM + follow-ups + AI agent: the API pays for itself quickly — every rescued prospect covers the month's bill.

The recommended path for an Ivorian SME

Start by squeezing everything out of the free app: full profile, catalog, automatic messages, labels. Measure your volume for two weeks. If you are overwhelmed — unanswered evening messages, lost prospects, team stepping on each other — that is the signal: have your sales flow audited before paying for anything. A good audit tells you whether the API is justified and, above all, what to plug into it so it pays. Our complete WhatsApp automation guide details the four possible levels.

Frequently asked questions

Is the WhatsApp Business API paid?

Yes. Meta charges per conversation (a few dozen FCFA depending on the message type), plus the cost of the platform or development that uses the API. The WhatsApp Business app, on the other hand, is entirely free.

Can I keep my number when moving to the API?

Yes, an existing number can be migrated to the WhatsApp Business API. Careful: once migrated, it can no longer be used in the classic app. Migration needs preparation — typically a step we supervise during a project.

Does a small shop need the API?

In most cases, no. A shop handling fewer than 20–30 conversations a day already gets a lot from the free app well configured. The API becomes relevant when volume exceeds what a human can track, or when you need a connected CRM and automatic follow-ups.

Digitalizing an insurance brokerage in Côte d'Ivoire: the guide

Insurance brokerage is a follow-up business: dozens or hundreds of contracts, each with its renewal date, its premium notices, its reminders, its documents. As long as everything fits in a spreadsheet and the founder's memory, it works — until the missed renewal, the client gone quietly to a competitor, the claim whose file cannot be found. Here is how the best-organized Ivorian brokerages digitalize, project by project.

Why brokerage is the ideal business to digitalize

Because almost everything in it is predictable and repetitive: contract renewal dates are known a year ahead, premium reminders always follow the same circuit, the documents requested are the same for each contract type. Everything predictable and repetitive automates beautifully — it is the dream business for a system that works on its own.

Project 1 — The contract base and the renewals wall

The foundation: every client, every contract, every renewal date in a single base (a suitable CRM, not a shared spreadsheet). The system alerts automatically at D-60, D-30 and D-7 before each renewal — and sends the client a personalized WhatsApp message at the right time. Direct result: no renewal ever forgotten, and retention going up.

Project 2 — Premium and document reminders

Unpaid premiums and incomplete files are the two holes in the bucket. Automatic reminder sequences take care of them: spaced, polite payment reminders, and a missing-documents list chased until complete. Your team only steps in when the client replies — everything else runs on its own.

Project 3 — WhatsApp as the firm's front desk

Your policyholders already write to you on WhatsApp. An automated agent can answer standard requests 24/7 — certificate, claim status, documents needed, appointment booking — and hand over to a human as soon as the case leaves the script. For the policyholder it is premium service; for you, hours of phone desk saved.

The sensitive point: confidentiality. Policyholder data is sensitive. Demand role-based access, data that remains your property, and a provider who signs a confidentiality commitment. It is a selection criterion on par with price.

What it changes, numbers in hand

The three measurable effects of a digitalized brokerage: a higher renewal rate (no missed deadlines), faster collections (systematic reminders), and the ability to grow without hiring proportionally — the system absorbs the volume. Where to start

Export your current portfolio (even imperfect), list the next 12 months of renewals, and measure one week of time spent on manual reminders. Those three numbers are enough to build a plan. Frequently asked questions

What is the first digital project for a brokerage?

The renewals file. Centralizing all contracts with their renewal dates in a system that automatically alerts 60, 30 and 7 days ahead is the fastest-paying project: every missed renewal is a lost premium, often to a competitor.

Can insurance customer data be entrusted to a provider?

Yes, with precautions: a confidentiality agreement, role-based access, and tools where the data remains your property. These are normal requirements to put to any provider — and a good provider offers them unprompted.

How long does digitalizing a brokerage take?

The foundation — centralized contract base, renewal alerts, automatic reminders — is set up in a few weeks. The next projects (client portal, e-signature, dashboards) are added progressively, one at a time.

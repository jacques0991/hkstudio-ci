05 — Technical process

Our Method

Discover how we work to audit, prototype, integrate, and monitor your automation architectures.

01 / CADRER

Frame and Audit

We analyze your current workflows and software (CRM, ERP, Excel sheets) to identify wasted time and scope the project with a guaranteed return on investment.

Concretely

2–3 on-site workshops, a map of your current flows and a quantified audit report. Deliverable: a prioritized roadmap showing, for each automation, the estimated time saved and the cost. Duration: about 1 week.

02 / CONCEVOIR

Design Workflows

We model the complete technical diagram (Workflow chart) connecting databases, communication tools, and AI models. You validate the functional prototype before development.

Concretely

We draw the technical blueprint of each scenario — triggers, conditions, connections — and a clickable prototype. You validate it on a real case before a single line goes to production.

03 / CONSTRUIRE

Integrate APIs

Our engineers securely deploy scenarios (Make, n8n, CRM). We connect accounts using encrypted API keys and access tokens and conduct load testing.

Concretely

Deployment on your accounts, secure connections via encrypted API keys, then a testing phase on real data (load, error cases). Nothing goes live until the success rate reaches 99%.

04 / DÉPLOYER

Monitor and Support

We deploy connectors to production, train your teams to ensure autonomy, and conduct technical monitoring with error alerts in case of API failures.

Concretely

Gradual go-live, team training (with documentation) and continuous monitoring. If an API fails, an automatic alert notifies us and we step in — often before you even notice.
